## Personas
- **Account holder** — a signed-in user who wants to stay informed.

## Epic: Alerts

### US-1: Receive an email on password change
**As an** account holder, **I want to** receive an email when my password changes **so that I can** spot unauthorised access.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Given my password is changed, when the change is saved, then an email is sent to the account address.

**Priority:** High
