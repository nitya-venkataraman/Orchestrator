## Epic: Bookmarks

### US-1: Bookmark an article
**As a** reader, **I want to** bookmark an article **so that I can** return to it later.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Given I am reading an article, when I tap the bookmark icon, then it is added to my bookmarks list.
- Given an article is already bookmarked, when I tap the icon again, then it is removed.

**Priority:** Medium
