## Personas
- **Registered member** — a signed-in account holder personalising their profile.

## Epic: Profile

### US-1: Upload a profile picture
**As a** registered member, **I want to** upload a profile picture **so that I can** be recognised by other members.

**Acceptance criteria**
- [ ] PNG and JPEG files up to 5 MB are accepted.
- [ ] Files over 5 MB or of other types are rejected with a clear message.
- [ ] The new picture appears within 2 seconds without a full page reload.

**Priority:** Low
